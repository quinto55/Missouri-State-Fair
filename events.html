New at the Fair

Governor Michael L. Parson Arena

The fairgrounds’ newest landmark opened at the 2026 Fair. It’s a climate-controlled, multipurpose arena built to host livestock and other events all year long — and Former Governor Parson and Mrs. Theresa Parson cut the ribbon on Opening Day.

2026 arena recap · 2027 arena events announced closer to the Fair

  • A three-day rodeo was the arena’s very first event.
  • Fan-favorite zebra, camel and ostrich races — plus jousting — came next.
  • A two-night bull ride closed out the Fair.
