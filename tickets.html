Fair admission

One gate ticket, the whole grounds

A single-day ticket gets you into the barns, exhibit buildings, free stages and family fun. Buy ahead online, or pick one up at the gate on Fair day.

2026 gate prices shown · 2027 advance tickets on sale in July

Single-day admission · 2026 prices

With online fees: adult up to $15.56 · senior up to $10.46 · youth up to $4.33.

  • Prices are for one day of Fair admission.
  • Advance adult admission goes on sale online in July; regular daily tickets are sold on site during the Fair.
  • Gates open 7:30 am – 10 pm every day (use Gate 6 after 10 pm).

Heads up: Grandstand concert and motorsport tickets do not include Fair admission — you’ll need a gate ticket too.

Exhibitor admission

Showing livestock or entering a contest? Exhibitors can buy discounted admission when they enter.

  • Limit of 40 tickets per exhibitor.
  • One season pass wristband equals 11 days of exhibitor admission.
  • Youth exhibitors 12 & under get a free admission wristband when their date of birth is entered correctly on their entries.
  • Exhibitor admission doesn’t include exhibitor parking.

Sensory-friendly midway

A calmer, more inclusive midway from noon to 2 pm, so everyone can enjoy the rides.

Fri, Aug 14 & Thu, Aug 20, 2026

Ride safety comes first. The Fair follows every Missouri Department of Public Safety regulation and welcomes state inspectors on the midway. On top of that, a nationally certified third-party ride inspector is on the grounds for all 11 days of the Fair. See state ride regulations.
