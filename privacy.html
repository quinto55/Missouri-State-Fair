Privacy

Short version: this concept site doesn’t track you.

This is a redesign concept prepared for the Missouri State Fair. It is not the official Fair website and does not sell tickets or collect personal information.

What this site does — and doesn’t — do

  • No cookies. The site sets no cookies of any kind.
  • No trackers or analytics. There are no analytics scripts, ad pixels, tag managers or third-party embeds.
  • No forms that send data. Nothing you do here is submitted to a server.
  • Photos and logos come from mostatefair.com. Images are loaded directly from the official Missouri State Fair website, so your browser makes standard requests to that server when you view a page.
  • Offline copy stays on your device. A service worker saves the site’s pages, styles and fonts in your browser’s cache so it loads fast and works offline. That cache never leaves your device, and clearing site data removes it.
  • Links leave this site. Buttons for alerts, applications, news and social media take you to the official Fair site or other services, which have their own policies.

The official privacy policy

For the Missouri State Fair’s own privacy practices, see the official policy on mostatefair.com.
