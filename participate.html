Livestock shows & building exhibits

Bring your best to Sedalia

Every ribbon at the Fair starts with an entry. Livestock shows cover cattle, swine, sheep, goats, poultry, rabbits, mules, donkeys and horses, while the competitive buildings welcome bakers, gardeners, artists and more competing for the same purple ribbon.

  • Premium Guide — the ultimate guide to State Fair competitions, available starting in April.
  • Exhibitor admission is bought with your entry form or from the Business Office ahead of the Fair — it is not sold at the gate.
  • Results are typically posted within 2–3 days once judging begins.

Vendors & concessionaires

Set up shop at the Fair

Any individual 18 or older, organization or business may apply. Fair management builds the most interesting mix of exhibits and concessions it can, with an emphasis on agriculture, diversity, high quality and products fairgoers love.

  1. Apply early. The application is posted by February and accepted up until the Fair begins. Include photos of your proposed booth and every product or service you plan to sell.
  2. Review begins April 1. Space isn’t first-come, first-served — prior-year exhibitors in good standing usually get first right of refusal.
  3. Hear back. Invitations go out any time up to a week before the Fair. Please don’t call to check status — Concessions staff will contact you if selected.
  4. Get insured. A certificate naming “Missouri State Fair as additional insured” must be on file before deadlines.
  5. Reapply each year. Applications aren’t held over.
